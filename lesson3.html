What You Will Learn

  • Understanding project settings, layers and timeline
  • Animating position, scale and rotation with keyframes
  • Text and shape animation
  • Effects, masking, blending and grouping
  • Exporting a smooth video

Step-by-step guide

  1. Create a project and choose its aspect ratio, resolution and frame rate.
  2. Add media, text or shapes as separate layers; rename layers to stay organized.
  3. Select a layer and set a starting keyframe for position, scale or rotation.
  4. Move the playhead and change the property to create the next keyframe.
  5. Preview the motion; adjust timing and easing for smoother movement.
  6. Experiment with one effect or a mask, then check layer order and opacity.
  7. Export the project and review the saved video.
Pro tip: Save a project copy before trying complicated effects. Keep transitions purposeful and preview your edit before exporting.

Practical Challenge

ZERO TO PRO • 8 STAGES

Alight Motion: Beginner to Pro Editor

Motion design path from layer basics and keyframes to polished animation, compositing and portfolio work.

Stage 1 — Beginner: Project & Layers

Set canvas, aspect ratio and frame rate; understand layers, groups and timeline.

Stage 2 — Keyframe Fundamentals

Animate position, scale, rotation, opacity; learn timing, easing and graph curves.

Stage 3 — Text & Shape Animation

Create kinetic typography, shape transitions, animated logos and reusable design elements.

Stage 4 — Effects & Compositing

Use masks, blending, effects, layer parenting/grouping and visual hierarchy.

Stage 5 — Advanced Motion

Practice motion curves, overshoot, follow-through, camera/parallax concepts and seamless loops.

Stage 6 — Tracking & Visual Polish

Combine footage and graphics, refine edges, manage color and keep effects consistent.

Stage 7 — Export & Optimization

Choose export settings, frame rate and quality; check smoothness, artifacts and file playback.

Stage 8 — Pro Motion Portfolio

Create a branded intro, lyric/kinetic typography clip and a polished 15–30 second motion edit.

Pro-Level Portfolio Challenge

Complete a polished project that demonstrates the skills above. Keep your project organized, preview it fully, and check the final export.
