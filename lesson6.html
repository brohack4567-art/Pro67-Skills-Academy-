LEARNING ROADMAP

0 to Pro: 8 Stages

Stage 1 — Beginner: Setup & Interface

  • Install and launch Resolve; understand project manager and database/project files.
  • Set timeline resolution, frame rate, playback settings and media organization.
  • Understand Media, Cut, Edit, Fusion, Color, Fairlight and Deliver pages.

Stage 2 — Editing Fundamentals

  • Import and organize footage using bins, metadata and smart workflow.
  • Practice source viewer, timeline viewer, insert/overwrite, ripple, roll, slip and slide edits.
  • Use keyboard shortcuts, markers, snapping, J-K-L playback and trim precision.
  • Build a clear sequence with pacing, continuity and story structure.

Stage 3 — Intermediate Editing & Finishing

  • Multicam editing, proxies/optimized media, compound clips and nested timelines.
  • Titles, subtitles/captions, transitions, speed changes and retiming controls.
  • Sync dialogue, manage tracks, use buses and keep audio levels consistent.

Stage 4 — Color Correction & Grading

  • Scopes: waveform, parade, vectorscope; exposure, contrast, white balance and saturation.
  • Node-based grading: serial/parallel nodes, qualifiers, power windows and tracking.
  • Shot matching, skin-tone consistency, LUTs used carefully and creative looks.
  • Understand color management, timeline/output color space and HDR basics.

Stage 5 — Fusion: Motion Graphics & VFX

  • Node graph basics: MediaIn, Merge, Transform, Text+, MediaOut.
  • Keyframes, spline editor, easing, masks, tracking and compositing.
  • Green-screen keying, cleanup, title animation and simple 2.5D effects.

Stage 6 — Fairlight: Professional Audio

  • Dialogue cleanup, EQ, compression, noise reduction where available, and automation.
  • Balance dialogue, music and sound effects; use buses and loudness meters.
  • Build transitions and sound design that support the edit.

Stage 7 — Advanced Workflow & Delivery

  • Proxy/optimized workflow, relinking media, versioning and project backups.
  • Render settings, codecs/containers, resolution, frame rate, bitrate and audio format.
  • Deliver platform-ready masters; check exports for sync, clipping, black frames and artifacts.

Stage 8 — Pro Portfolio & Client Workflow

  • Make a polished short film, a promo/ad, a color-grade comparison and a motion-graphics sample.
  • Use briefs, references, revision notes, organized project handoff and final QC.
  • Develop story sense, visual consistency, intentional effects and dependable deadlines.
PRACTICE PLAN

Project Challenges

  1. Beginner: 30-second montage with clean cuts, title and music.
  2. Intermediate: 60-second mini story with dialogue cleanup, captions and pacing.
  3. Color: match 5 shots, correct exposure/white balance and create a consistent look.
  4. Fusion: animate a 5–8 second title with tracking or masking.
  5. Advanced: create a 60–90 second promo with sound design, color grade and platform-ready exports.
