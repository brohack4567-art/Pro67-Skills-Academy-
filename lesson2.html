What You Will Learn

  • Project setup and aspect ratio
  • Importing footage and arranging timeline clips
  • Cut, trim, split and layer editing
  • Titles, transitions, audio and effects
  • Export resolution and frame rate

Step-by-step guide

  1. Open KineMaster and create a project with the correct aspect ratio.
  2. Import clips, then drag them into the required order on the timeline.
  3. Use trim and split to remove unwanted moments; keep the pacing clear.
  4. Add text as a layer and adjust its timing, position and animation.
  5. Add music or voice-over, then balance audio levels.
  6. Use a transition or effect only where it supports the story.
  7. Preview the complete edit and export in a suitable resolution and frame rate.
Pro tip: Save a project copy before trying complicated effects. Keep transitions purposeful and preview your edit before exporting.

Practical Challenge

Create a 30-second video with three clips, a title, one transition and background music. Export it and check the final playback.
